## Week 1 posting rules
- Post at the same time daily while you find your peak window — test 8-10am first, adjust after day 3 if data says otherwise.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour, every day, no exceptions — this is what the algorithm actually rewards, more than the video itself.
- Do NOT swing for a viral/breakout attempt this week — this week is about establishing a clean baseline (avg views, avg hook-rate) so week 3's breakout swings have something real to beat.
- Track daily: views, average watch %, saves, comments, follower delta. Log it in the dashboard (see `dashboard/index.html`).
