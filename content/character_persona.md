# On-Camera Character & AI Image Prompt — "The AI Automation Founder"

Use this for a consistent talking-head persona across every reel (same face, same energy, same set).

---

## 1. Character definition (who he is)

- **Name/role:** Founder of a premium AI automation agency. The "quietly confident operator" who fixes businesses' hidden money leaks.
- **Age/look:** Indian male, late 20s to early 30s. Sharp, clean, modern. Short well-groomed black hair, neat stubble, warm brown skin, expressive dark eyes, natural confident half-smile.
- **Personality on camera:** blunt but likeable, high-status but approachable, no hype — he talks like he's letting you in on something. Direct eye contact, calm hands, leans in slightly on key lines.
- **Vibe reference:** think "young tech founder who just closed a big deal" — expensive-simple, not flashy.

## 2. Wardrobe (premium-minimal)

- **Primary look:** fitted plain charcoal or black crew-neck tee under an unstructured tailored blazer (deep navy or charcoal). Sleeves slightly pushed. Minimal silver watch.
- **Alt look:** crisp fitted white or black button-down, top button open, sleeves rolled once.
- Keep it solid colors, no logos, no busy patterns — reads clean on small screens.

## 3. Background / set

- Modern minimal office or studio: soft-blurred bokeh of a dark room with warm accent lights and faint tech glow (monitor light, LED strip). A hint of shelves/plants out of focus.
- Shallow depth of field so HE pops and the background melts. Nothing distracting behind his head.

## 4. Color grading

- **Cinematic teal-and-orange:** warm skin tones, slightly teal shadows, gentle contrast, soft film grain. Premium, moody, "Apple-ad meets creator" look.
- Slightly crushed blacks, warm highlight roll-off. Not oversaturated.

## 5. Lighting / camera

- Soft key light 45° camera-left, subtle rim light on the shoulder/hair to separate from background.
- Vertical 9:16, eye-level, ~35–50mm equivalent, subject framed chest-up with headroom for on-screen text.

---

```

cartoon, 3d render, plastic skin, extra fingers, deformed hands, logos, text, watermark,
```

**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Style:** photoreal / cinematic

---

## 7. Consistency tips (same character every time)

- Lock a **seed number** (Midjourney/Flux) and reuse it so the face stays the same across shots.
- Or generate one strong hero image, then use it as an **image reference / character reference** for future prompts.
- Keep the same wardrobe + background lines in every prompt; only change pose/expression per reel mood (e.g., "arms crossed, serious" for hot-take reels, "leaning in, warm" for story reels).

## Variations per reel mood
- **Story reel (today's dentist reel):** "leaning slightly forward, warm empathetic expression, softer warm grade."
- **Hot-take reel (n8n is dead):** "arms crossed, confident smirk, slightly harder contrast, cooler shadows."
- **Money/offer reel ($5k/mo):** "relaxed, hands open mid-gesture, bright confident smile."
