## 0. The honest math first

100K in 90 days from zero is a *stretch goal*, not a baseline expectation. Here's why, and what it actually takes:

- Pure organic, consistent posting in this niche realistically gets a disciplined new account to **10-30K in 90 days**.
- Accounts that hit 100K that fast almost always have **one thing**: a single reel that breaks past 1-5M views and triggers a follow snowball, OR they're spending on paid boost behind proven winners, OR they had an existing audience elsewhere (YouTube/TikTok) to seed from.
- So the plan below is built in 3 layers, stacked:
  1. **Volume layer** (organic, non-negotiable, compounds slowly)
  2. **Breakout-engineering layer** (deliberately swinging for 1-2 viral attempts/week using proven patterns from your own scrape data)
  3. **Paid amplification layer** (small budget behind anything that shows early breakout signal — this is what actually gets stretch goals hit)

If you skip layer 3 entirely, treat 20-40K as the realistic 90-day ceiling and 100K as the upside case.

## 1. Account setup (before day 1)

- **New handle**, not a rename of your personal one — clean slate, no 2000-following baggage.
- **Bio:** identity + proof + CTA in 3 lines. Reuse the pattern from `content/account_positioning.md`:

- **Following count starts at 0 and stays low.** Only follow accounts you're genuinely studying/collabing with. Do not repeat the 2000-following mistake — a 0-follower account following 500 people looks like a bot/spam account and actively hurts follow-back rate.
- **First 3 posts pinned before any promotion:** one proof/demo reel, one founder-story reel, one big-idea/AI-shift reel. This is what a first-time visitor sees — it has to sell the account in 3 videos.

## 2. Volume layer — the floor that makes everything else possible

Growth math: virality is a probability game. More qualified attempts = more shots at a breakout.

- **2 reels/day minimum**, 7 days/week → ~180 reels over 90 days.
- 1 carousel or story-set/day repurposing that day's reel hook (cheap extra surface area).
- Every reel scripted before filming — no winging it. Use `content/hook_bank.md` + `content/trending_topics.md` as the hook/topic source, not raw invention.
- **Repurpose everything to TikTok + YouTube Shorts same day.** Same video, same captions, near-zero extra effort, and it's free additional discovery surface that can also loop views/followers back to IG via bio link. Cross-posting doesn't cost you anything and roughly triples your shots at an algorithm picking the video up somewhere.

## 3. Breakout-engineering layer — deliberately swinging for viral

This is what separates 20K from 100K. From your own 40-post scrape (`research/2026-07-09_ai-automation/report.html`), the patterns that actually produced 100K+ views were specific, not random:

- **Contrarian/shock hook** (@joestoltelive, 468K) — flip a common belief hard in the first 2 seconds.
- **Breaking-news framing** (@getintoai, 178K) — "X just happened" urgency.
- **Comment-bait CTA** — present in 3 of the top 6 posts. Every reel needs one keyword CTA ("Comment BOND", "Comment STACK").

**Weekly breakout attempts:** pick 2 topics per week specifically for viral swing (not safe/educational content) — a contrarian take on something everyone in AI has an opinion on, or a live newsjack on a story breaking that week (see the newsjack section in `content/trending_topics.md` for the live pattern this already worked with once — refresh that list weekly).

**Do this every time you post one of these swing reels:**
1. Post at your tested peak time.
2. Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes — this is the single highest-leverage 60 minutes of the post's life for the algorithm.
3. If it crosses 3x your average reel's 1-hour view count, that's your breakout-engineering signal — move to layer 4 immediately, don't wait.

## 4. Paid amplification layer — the actual 100K lever

Organic + breakout attempts alone rarely compound fast enough for 100K in 90 days. The lever that does it:

- Set aside a **small daily boost budget** (even $10-20/day) specifically to put behind content that's already showing organic pull in its first few hours — never boost cold, unproven content.
- Meta's "Boost post" targeting on a video already outperforming your average is the highest-ROI use of ad spend you have, because you're paying to amplify a signal that already exists, not guessing.
- If a video hits breakout signal (step above) and you have budget, put $50-100 behind it within the first 24 hours while it's still climbing — this is the single biggest thing that turns a good week into a 100K month.
- This requires a real decision from you: **is there ad budget for this, and how much?** Without any paid layer, treat 100K as aspirational and 20-40K as the target.

## 6. 90-day arc

- **Days 1-14 — Foundation.** Post volume starts day 1. No swinging for breakout yet — build a baseline of what your normal reel does (views/retention) so you can spot a real breakout signal later. Pin your 3 best.
- **Days 15-45 — Breakout swings begin.** 2 deliberate viral-attempt reels/week. Track hook-rate and 1-hour view velocity on everything. First real newsjacks. This is where most of your growth curve gets decided.
- **Days 46-75 — Double down + paid layer active.** Whatever pattern popped in phase 2, run it again 3-4x with variations. Boost budget goes behind anything showing early breakout signal, fast. Start 1-2 creator collabs/shoutout swaps in the same niche (see below) — this is a second real lever for a jump in followers, not just organic reach.
- **Days 76-90 — Compound + convert.** Keep volume up. Shift a bit more toward case-study/proof content to make sure the audience you built actually has a reason to stick around and (eventually) become agency leads, not just a vanity number.

## 7. Second real lever: collabs, not just solo posting

Solo organic + paid boost is layers 2-4. The other proven fast-follower-count lever is **borrowing someone else's audience**:
- Instagram "Collab" posts with 1-2 similarly-sized AI/automation creators (posts to both feeds, doubles the reach surface for one piece of content).
- Shoutout-for-shoutout swaps with accounts in the 10-50K range in adjacent niches (AI tools, automation, SaaS founders) — mutual, no cost, real follower transfer.
- Guest appearances (duet/stitch a popular AI creator's take with your own angle) — rides their reach without needing their permission.

Not scripted here in detail because it depends on who responds — but budget outreach time for this starting week 3-4, once you have enough posted content to make the swap worth it for the other side.
