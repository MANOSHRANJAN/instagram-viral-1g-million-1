## 3. Content pillars (rotate these)

Two jobs: **REACH pillars** pull in a broad AI-curious audience (grow followers fast); **CONVERT pillars** turn them into agency leads. You need both — reach content is what actually gets you to 10K.

| Pillar | Type | % | Why it grows you | Example |
|--------|------|---|------------------|---------|
| **AI shifts & big ideas** | REACH | 25% | broad appeal, shares, "wait what" — pulls non-buyers | "This AI update just changed every business overnight" |
| **Tool & automation breakdowns** | REACH | 20% | rides trends, high saves | "5 AI tools that replace a whole team" |
| **Proof / demos** | CONVERT | 20% | AI audience wants "show me it works" | screen-rec of the receptionist booking a real appt |
| **Contrarian hot takes** | REACH | 15% | comment-drivers = reach | "Human receptionists are a luxury now" |
| **Case studies / results** | CONVERT | 10% | trust + saves → converts | "214 appointments, zero humans" |
| **Founder / build-in-public** | BRAND | 10% | builds the personal brand + trust | "I built an AI agency with zero coding" |

**Rule of thumb:** ~60% reach/education (talk about AI + how it changes work/life/business), ~30% proof/convert, ~10% you. Educate broadly, sell narrowly.

## 4. Format rules (non-negotiable, from what's working)

- **Hook in the first 1.7 seconds** — visual + spoken. No intros.
- **Trending audio** under every reel (even talking-head — low volume bed).
- **Every reel scripted** before recording (we do this together).
- **Burned-in captions** always (silent autoplay).
- **7–30s** length. Retention > length.
- **One CTA keyword** per reel ("BOT", "LEAK", "STACK") → your DM bot.
- Talking-head + light B-roll OR pure story cut (like today's dentist reel).
