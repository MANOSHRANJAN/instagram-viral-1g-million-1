## DELIVERY & PACING (this replaces heavy editing)

- Shoot **one continuous take**, phone at eye level, you slightly leaning in. Natural room, soft window light.
- Talk to ONE person, not a camera. Conversational, like you're telling a mate over coffee.
- **Pauses are the edit.** Beat of silence after: "unlock his phone" … "he went quiet" … "how long was I losing this."
- Speed up slightly during the math (0:14–0:26) — build pressure. Then **slow right down** on the last two lines.
- Keep your hands visible; one honest gesture on "hundred-thousand-dollar leak."

## MINIMAL VISUALS (only 3 — don't over-edit)

1. **0:04** — quick cut-in of a phone screen showing a wall of "Missed Call" notifications. 1s, then back to you.
2. **0:11** — plain on-screen number appears: `$100,000 / month`. No animation, just snaps in.
3. **0:23** — a calendar filling with appointments. 1s, back to your face for the CTA.

Everything else = **you, talking.** That's the point.

## Why the hook is unskippable
"A dentist told me AI automation was a scam. So I asked him to unlock his phone." — It's a mini-cliffhanger. You HAVE to know what was on the phone. No stat, no intro, straight into a story with a person and a mystery.

## Why the CTA converts (and feels un-skippable)
It's tied to the story's emotion ("I can't watch good businesses bleed"), it's a single word ("LEAK"), it's free, and it explicitly says "not selling" — which removes the guard that makes people scroll past CTAs.
