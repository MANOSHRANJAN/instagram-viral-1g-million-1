## WHAT A "DOPAMINE LOOP" ACTUALLY IS (so the structure makes sense)

A dopamine loop is a chain of **small open loops** where each answer plants the next question — the brain can't look away because it's mid-anticipation the whole time, not just once at the hook.

```
OPEN LOOP 1 (hook)
   ↓ tease of an answer → OPENS LOOP 2
   ↓ tease of an answer → OPENS LOOP 3
   ↓ BIG REVEAL (closes all loops at once — the dopamine spike)
   ↓ NEW LOOP (the CTA) → carries the craving into the comments
```

This is why the script below never just "answers" a question — every payoff plants the next hook mid-sentence.

## DELIVERY NOTES (this replaces heavy editing)

- **One continuous take**, eye-level, direct to camera — you're telling this to one person, not performing for a crowd.
- **The loop-opens get a slight forward lean + raised energy** ("something bigger just quietly shipped" / "before: forty-seven missed calls... gone").
- **The big reveal (0:28) gets a beat of silence before it** — let "After?" hang for a full second. That silence IS the dopamine set-up.
- **Slow down on the last line of the reveal** ("because the business finally had something working behind it while he slept") — this is the payoff, don't rush it.
- **CTA delivered calm, almost offhand** — confidence, not begging.
