# 🎬 Viral Script: The "Stop Doing X" Myth-Buster
**Niche:** AI Websites & Automation for Local Businesses
**Target Audience:** Service business owners who are wasting money on bad marketing
**Goal:** Agitate a pain point and drive DMs for a free consultation/audit

## 1. Visual Hook (First 3 seconds)
*You are holding a piece of paper that says "$2,000/mo SEO Agency" and you literally crumple it up and throw it at the camera.*
**On-Screen Text:** Stop wasting money on SEO. Do this instead.

## 2. The Verbal Hook (0:00 - 0:05)
**You (Aggressive, confident):**
"Stop paying marketing agencies $2,000 a month just to get you 5 leads that never answer the phone. The game has completely changed."

## 3. The Setup & Agitation (0:05 - 0:15)
*B-Roll: You walking down the street or sitting in a nice office, holding the camera selfie-style to make it feel raw and personal.*
**You:**
"Most local businesses think they need better SEO or more Facebook ads. You don't. You have a massive leak in your bucket. When someone actually visits your site, they fill out a form, and then they wait 4 hours for you to call them back. By the time you call, they already hired the guy down the street."

## 4. The Value Drop & Solution (0:15 - 0:30)
*Screen record: Fast-paced demonstration of a sleek AI chat widget on a website intercepting a lead.*
**You:**
"I build AI websites that fix this instantly. My system installs an AI agent on your site that responds to every single inquiry in under 3 seconds. It asks them about their problem, gives them a rough estimate, and books them onto your calendar automatically."

## 5. The Proof & CTA (0:30 - 0:45)
*Screen record: A split screen showing a traditional boring contact form crossed out in red (❌) vs your AI chat interface checked in green (✅).*
**You:**
"I deployed this for a roofing client last week, and his closing rate doubled overnight just because he was the first one to respond. If you want to stop losing money and actually convert your website traffic, comment the word **LEAK** below, and I’ll send you a video showing exactly how it works."

---

### 📦 Assets to Prepare:
1. **The Prop:** A piece of paper with "$2,000/mo SEO" written on it in thick sharpie.
2. **The B-Roll:** Selfie-style walking footage (high energy).
3. **The Demo:** A recording of you interacting with a web chatbot that acts like a human (e.g., using Voiceflow, Botpress, or Bland AI).
4. **Caption:** "Are you bleeding leads? 🩸 Every minute you wait to respond to a customer, your chance of closing drops by 80%. An AI website guarantees a 3-second response time. ⏱️ Comment LEAK and I'll send you the exact system I use to plug the holes in local businesses."
