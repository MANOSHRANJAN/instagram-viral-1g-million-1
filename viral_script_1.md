# 🎬 Viral Script: The "I Built This" Format (Nick Saraev Style)
**Niche:** AI Websites & Automation for Local Businesses
**Target Audience:** Small business owners (plumbers, roofers, HVAC)
**Goal:** Book an appointment/Call to Action

## 1. Visual Hook (First 3 seconds)
*The video starts mid-action. You are pointing at your laptop screen showing a wildly complex-looking automation flow (n8n or Make.com) that suddenly turns green/successful.*
**On-Screen Text:** "Your website is losing you $10k/mo. Here's why."

## 2. The Verbal Hook (0:00 - 0:05)
**You (Talking fast, high energy):**
"If you run a local business, your $3,000 website is basically a digital business card that does absolutely nothing. I'll prove it to you right now."

## 3. The Setup & Proof (0:05 - 0:20)
*Screen record: switch to a sleek, modern AI-powered website template.*
**You:**
"I built this entire website for a plumbing client in less than 30 minutes using AI. But it’s not just a pretty page." 

*Screen record: switch back to the automation flow, zooming in on the 'Twilio' and 'OpenAI' nodes.*
**You:**
"Behind the scenes, I connected it to a custom AI agent. When a customer fills out the contact form at 2 AM, the AI instantly texts them back, qualifies the lead, and books the appointment directly into my client's calendar while he's literally sleeping."

## 4. The Value Drop (0:20 - 0:35)
*B-Roll: You looking at the camera, holding up your phone showing a calendar full of booked calls.*
**You:**
"While your competitors are letting leads go to voicemail and losing deals, my clients have a 24/7 sales rep that costs less than their monthly coffee budget."

## 5. The CTA (0:35 - 0:45)
*Fast cut: Back to you, direct eye contact.*
**You:**
"Stop paying agencies thousands of dollars for websites that don't convert. If you want me to build this exact AI system for your business, comment the word **SYSTEM** down below, and I'll send you a free breakdown of how it works."

---

### 📦 Assets to Prepare:
1. **Background Flow:** A dummy n8n or Make.com scenario (just needs to look complex and technical).
2. **The Website Demo:** Any sleek Framer or Webflow template you've built.
3. **The Proof:** A screenshot of a Google Calendar or GoHighLevel pipeline packed with appointments. 
4. **Caption:** "Stop letting leads slip through the cracks. 💸 The old way: Pay $3k for a static website. Wait for calls. Lose leads to voicemail. 📉 The new way: AI websites that automatically qualify and book leads 24/7. 📈 Comment SYSTEM and I’ll send you the playbook."
