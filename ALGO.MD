This video, created by Kallaway, breaks down how social media algorithms function as "matchmakers" to keep users on platforms, and provides actionable tactics for creators to optimize their content for growth.

How Algorithms Actually Work (0:43 - 5:21)
Core Objective: The primary goal of any social algorithm is to maximize the time users spend on the platform to increase ad revenue.
The Process:
Topic Mapping: When you post, the platform performs a multimodal analysis (visuals, audio, transcript, and metadata) to understand the content.
The "Sample Test": The video is shown to an initial group of roughly 200 people (mostly non-followers) to gauge performance.
Boosting vs. Stopping: Positive engagement metrics from this sample lead to larger pushes (2,000, 20,000, etc.), while weak data causes the algorithm to stop promoting the video.
How to Hijack the Algorithm (5:21 - 15:46)
Improve Sample Fit (Audience Matching):
Consistently create content within a narrow niche for a specific avatar to help the algorithm identify exactly who your ideal viewer is.
Avoid "spraying and praying" across different topics, as this confuses the algorithm and leads to poor sample data.
Increase Engagement:
To drive better metrics (watch time, engagement rate, session share), ensure your content:
Is relevant to the viewer's core pain points.
Provides non-obvious, actionable information.
Is easy to absorb and understand.
Offers a short distance to implementation.
Driving Comments: To boost algorithmic push, take a hard, contrarian stance on popular topics, use extreme framing, and discuss "cult-loved" brands or movements to trigger emotional responses.
Summary: Kallaway emphasizes that successful growth comes from focusing on the "cake"—making high-quality content for a consistent audience—rather than chasing "icing" like posting times, specific hashtags, or caption hacks.
